# Third-party sample assets

These assets are included only to demonstrate PreviewDock's file viewers.

## Office and diagrams

- `office/product-presentation.ppt` and the `.dps` compatibility copy are based on
  Apache POI's `test-data/slideshow/SampleShow.ppt` fixture.
- `diagrams/process-overview.vsdx` is Apache POI's
  `test-data/diagram/github260.vsdx` hotel-reservation flow fixture.

Source: https://github.com/apache/poi

License: Apache License 2.0.

## Images

The files under `images/` are format conversions of NASA's Blue Marble image.

Source: https://swot.jpl.nasa.gov/resources/122/nasas-blue-marble/

Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech. See the linked NASA/JPL image policy for reuse terms.

## Audio and video

- `media/preview-tone.*` is converted from MDN's CC0 T-Rex roar example.
- `media/product-tour.*` is copied or converted from MDN's CC0 flower video.

Sources:

- https://interactive-examples.mdn.mozilla.net/media/cc0-audio/t-rex-roar.mp3
- https://interactive-examples.mdn.mozilla.net/media/cc0-videos/flower.mp4
- https://interactive-examples.mdn.mozilla.net/media/cc0-videos/flower.webm

License: CC0, as identified by MDN's example asset collection.

## 3D and CAD

The following model files are copied from the Three.js example library:

- `models/product-demo.fbx` from `examples/models/fbx/Samba Dancing.fbx`
- `models/product-demo.3ds` from `examples/models/3ds/portalgun/portalgun.3ds`
- `models/product-demo.glb` from `examples/models/gltf/Flamingo.glb`
- `models/product-cylinder.brep` from `data/occ/solid.brep` in Open CASCADE Technology

Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

BREP source: https://github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT

Licenses: Three.js is MIT licensed. Open CASCADE Technology is licensed under
LGPL-2.1 with the OCCT exception.

All remaining files in this directory are generated specifically for PreviewDock.
