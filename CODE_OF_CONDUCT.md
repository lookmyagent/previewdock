# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, constructive
experience.

Be considerate, discuss technical disagreement in good faith, and avoid
harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing another person's
private information. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and
community participation that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers once the public repository
publishes its contact channel.
